## Based People Model

Based People Model is a community to celebrate it's members wins.

It has a focus on encouraging people to become proficient in their crafts, hence the word "Based", and celebrate their technical wins.

We choose to replicate [the blueprint of Large People Model](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lpm-vc/) community, HQed in San Francisco 🌉, with the [same sets of values](https://sakom.substack.com/p/lpm-large-people-model).

### Archetypes We Celebrate

✅ High Skilled → learn hard things fast and create elite output.

✅ Superstars → multipliers of impact, leading the way for others.

✅ Owners → embody entrepreneurial spirit, act without waiting for permission, and drive good-cause missions with vision and resources.

### What We Stand For - Non-Negotiable 5 Values

✅ Integrity - doing the right thing, even when no one’s watching.

✅ Doer - we act, we don’t wait.

✅ Giver - we share more than we take.

✅ Passion - we care deeply.

✅ Resilience - we keep going when it’s tough.

### How we operate

✅ Hiring: Review and add referrals fitting our 5 values: integrity, doer, giver, passion and resilience.

✅ Moderation: Make sure none is trying hard on selling and for quality

✅ Collaboration: Try to meet members and see what can help with. take pict and share in group, so others can connect later

✅ ⁠Education: Share things finding useful in events, in case folks can’t join benefit

✅ Promotion: Join fellow members’ events, promote their products, intro to your network etc.

### What we don’t value

✅ Credentials have an expiration date. Values don’t.

✅ Financial success isn’t sustainable if it’s not rooted in something deeper.

✅ No bureaucracy, no rules, no judgment.

✅ We don’t glorify busyness.

### What we value

✅ If someone asks for help → we care.

✅ How we feel.

✅ Keeping it short, kind and real.

✅ Whatever makes sense with right context and intention.

✅ Mastering right hook to trigger help.

✅ What we become: clarity, focus and depth.

### Where We’re Going

✅ We are not trying to be the biggest community.

✅ We’re trying to be the most aligned one.

✅ We’re competing only with our past version.

### A place where…

✅ Alignment on values isn’t a checkbox - it’s the foundation.

### How to Join

✅ It’s referral-only. Good people refer good people.

✅ We do a quick LinkedIn relevance check.

✅ We grow through trust, not gatekeeping. No vanity metrics. No “prove yourself first”. If we align on values, you are in.
